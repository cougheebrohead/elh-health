Audit chain on PHI access

Every read of member health data inserts a row into our audit chain with a SHA-256 digest of the previous row plus the current canonical event. Tampering with any historic row breaks verification at that point. Logs are retained for 7 years per HIPAA requirements and exportable on request.

Quarterly compliance reviews walk through the audit chain for your tenant and surface any anomalous access patterns.

Backup & restore

  • Encrypted backups every 24 hours, retained 30 days
  • Point-in-time recovery for the last 7 days
  • Weekly restore-test verified by automation
  • Disaster recovery RTO 4 hours, RPO 24 hours

Vulnerability disclosure

Coordinated disclosure to security@vitalstack.app. We respond within one business day and credit reporters in our public security log. We do not yet run a paid bounty program, but we will reward novel and impactful reports at our discretion.
