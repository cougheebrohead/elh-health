# ELH Health — enterprise health platform for gyms, clinics, multi-site
# org administrators. Healthcare-adjacent: data sensitivity is HIGH.
# We welcome reports from ethical security researchers.
#
# Reporting:
#   * Send a clear write-up to security@elhhealth.app.
#   * 90-day private window before public disclosure.
#   * For SUSPECTED PHI exposure: please mark the email subject with
#     [URGENT-PHI] so it routes to a same-day pager. We commit to
#     acknowledging PHI-exposure reports within 4 business hours.
#
# Eligible scope:
#   - https://elh-health.onrender.com/*
#   - https://elhhealth.app/*  (when zone live)
#   - The /api/* surface (REST, SCIM, SSO ACS)
#   - the elh-health-ios app once published
#
# Out of scope:
#   - Third-party platforms (Stripe, Supabase, Render, Cloudflare,
#     Anthropic, customer SAML IdPs)
#   - DoS / volumetric attacks
#   - Social engineering against staff or customer admins
#   - Physical access scenarios
#
# Safe Harbor:
#   Good-faith research consistent with this policy will not be
#   pursued legally. Stop at proof-of-concept; never access or modify
#   real org data (use your own test signup or our demo tenant).
#
# Bug bounty:
#   VDP-only currently. Will promote to bounty when revenue justifies.
#   Critical findings on PHI paths may receive recognition outside
#   the public scope.
