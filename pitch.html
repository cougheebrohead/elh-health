Section 04
The Four Cycle Phases — In Detail
Every female member's plan auto-adjusts across the cycle. Here's exactly how.

Every female member who opts in logs her cycle once. The platform tracks day-of-cycle and adjusts nutrition targets, workout intensity, recovery cues, and trainer messaging across all four phases. Trainers and club operators see phase context inline on every dashboard.

Days 1-5

Menstrual

Lower energy · iron focus · prioritize recovery

Energy dips, sleep quality drops, iron loss is real. Training intensity is auto-suggested low; recovery work and gentle mobility prioritized.

Auto-adjustments: calorie target unchanged · iron-rich food prompts · workout RPE capped 7 · sleep cue added.
Days 6-13

Follicular

Peak strength window · normal calories

Estrogen rising. Insulin sensitivity high. Best phase for strength PRs, hard intervals, body composition work.

Auto-adjustments: calorie target unchanged · workout RPE up to 9 · "strength window" prompt · carb timing prioritized around training.
Days 14-16

Ovulatory

Peak power · coordination · sociable energy

Brief testosterone peak. Best window for peak power lifts, athletic performance, coordination-demanding work.

Auto-adjustments: protein +5g · workout flagged for one-rep max attempts · "go for the PR" prompt sent.
Days 17-28

Luteal

Cravings · slower recovery · BMR up · cap intensity

Progesterone rises, BMR climbs ~5-10%, cravings real, recovery slower, sleep flightier. Most adherence breaks here — and where the platform earns its keep.

Auto-adjustments: calorie target +8% · protein +10g · complex-carb +20g · RPE capped 8 · "lean into the carb crave" trainer note · luteal-phase risk pill on console.

What this means for your chain

When a female member's adherence drops in late luteal — and the platform tells her trainer that's why, with the science to back it — the trainer messages her with a context-aware nudge instead of a panicked check-in. She doesn't feel judged. She doesn't ghost. She doesn't cancel. Most luteal-phase saves keep a member for 6+ more months.

Section 05
PCOS · Postpartum · Perimenopause
Three modules nobody else has built. All included in your license.

PCOS Protocol Module

Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome affects ~10% of women. Standard nutrition apps make it worse by ignoring insulin resistance. The PCOS module flags carb-heavy meals, prioritizes protein + fiber, and suggests training cadence aligned with managing insulin response.

  • Lower default carb target (~30-35% calories vs 45% standard)
  • Higher protein floor (~30% calories)
  • Inositol & spearmint tea prompts (research-backed)
  • Strength-priority training (vs cardio-heavy) for insulin sensitivity
  • HbA1c & fasting glucose tracking surfaced for the trainer

Postpartum Return-to-Training

The first 12 months postpartum are the highest-stakes retention window for any female member. The platform runs a 12-stage progression protocol — trainer-overridable — that gates training intensity until pelvic floor and core readiness pass milestones.

WindowDefault protocolTrainer can override
Weeks 0-6Walking only · breath work · pelvic-floor cues✓
Weeks 6-12Bodyweight reintroduction · core stabilization · zone-2 cardio✓
Weeks 12-24Progressive loading · split squats / hinges / pulls✓
Weeks 24-52Standard programming with periodized return to PR-chasing✓

Perimenopause & Menopause Module

~40 million U.S. women in peri/menopause — and the standard fitness platform ignores them entirely. The module rebalances calories, protein, training, and recovery for the new physiology: lower estrogen, slower recovery, lean-mass loss without intervention.

  • Higher protein floor (~35% calories) to preserve lean mass
  • Strength-priority training — minimum 3×/week heavy
  • HRT-aware nutrition (different requirements if on/off therapy)
  • Sleep + stress tracking elevated (peri sleep disturbance is real)
  • Bone-density check prompts at 6-month intervals
  • Hot-flash logging optional

Solid pattern, occasional gaps. Standard cadence — weekly check-in.

Slipping

50-75% adherence

Pattern is breaking. Reach out today — preferably with cycle-phase context if applicable.

Ghosting
